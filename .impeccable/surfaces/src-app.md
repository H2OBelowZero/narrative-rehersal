## Scope

Whole app (Welcome, Settings, project Home, Story, Rehearse, Crew, Discover). Mode: Operate. Phones first, expanding to tablet and desktop web. Direction pinned by the user's two mockups (approved comps); the concept roll was skipped because a brief-pinned direction beats it.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The app is the screenwriter's desk and its cork board. Every screen is a board of pinned paper, and the red string is both the story's causal chain and the navigation path. It refuses the category default of flat cards in a tab app.

OWN-WORLD: Warm near-black desk and walnut frame; cork field; aged index-card paper with Courier Prime type; masking-tape labels with Caveat handwriting; brass and red push pins; one accent, string red; noir photographs (generated, illustrative) pinned as prints. Chrome text in the system face.

STORY: The crew sees where the story stands, plays a moment, watches consequence pin itself to the board, and follows the string to discoveries and pages.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Phone: the project board. The wordmark and project sit on a dark header, the board fills the screen, and area cards are pinned and joined by one string. The string rail at the bottom is the navigation, with the active pin lit.

FORM: pinned by the user's comps (no roll; seed key: none, user-pinned).

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance

## Signature interaction

Following the string: switching sections animates a thread pulse travelling along the nav string from the old pin to the new one while the board slides in from that direction; within boards, strings connect cards in reading order.
